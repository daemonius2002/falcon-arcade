# Changelog

## v0.4.0

### Star Falcon v2.0
Star Falcon is now built from `tools/star-falcon/src` (the v1.2 core plus 24 feature modules) with
`node tools/star-falcon/build.js`. Old v1.2 saves load unchanged, with credits, upgrades and
unlocked stages kept.

- **Bosses:** a new boss at the end of every stage: Orbit Warden, Boulder Baron, Frost Monarch,
  Tidebreaker, Dune Driller, Magma Forge, The Prism and The Overseer. Each has its own attacks and
  phases, a named boss bar with phase pips, phase banners and a boss death show.
- **Boss Rush:** fight all 8 bosses back to back against the clock. It unlocks after you beat stage 8.
- **Wing Captain:** a mini-boss halfway through every stage.
- **Stage events:** shooting stars, meteor showers with splitting rocks, ice shard volleys and snow,
  and gunboats on stages 1-4. Warned hazards on stages 5-8: sandstorm gusts, lava geysers,
  lightning lanes and laser gates.
- **Enemies:** 5-ship squadrons on flight paths with a wipe bonus, gold Ace enemies and the Courier
  bonus ship.
- **Weapons:** Wing drones (a new Hangar item) and power capsules: spread, magnet, double cash and
  bubble shield.
- **Score:** an arcade score with a kill-chain multiplier up to x8, a close-call graze bonus and a
  best score per stage.
- **Results:** a stage tally with up to 3 stars, a Ship Down progress card, and stars and bests on
  the stage cards.
- **Music:** original chiptune music for every stage, the bosses, the menu and the Hangar, with
  jingles and separate Music and Effects volume.
- **Feel:** hit-stop, screen shake, impact sparks, a Mega bomb shockwave, ship hit flash, low-HP
  warning, respawn beam and engine trail.
- **Help for new pilots:** hints on stages 1-2, easy-to-see pink enemy bullets, optional bullet
  rings and a How to play screen.
- **Menus:** an Options screen, and a pause menu (Restart / Options / Menu) with a 3-2-1 countdown
  before play resumes.
- **Phones:** drag steering on touch, an instant BOMB button, a thumb bar during play (BOMB, Pause,
  Sound), and safe-area and landscape layout.
- **Balance:** retuned difficulty, boss HP matched to your firepower, new Hangar prices, and a
  "second wind" after repeated losses.

### Games
- **Air Hockey:** win the Falcon Cup against 5 rivals who get tougher, with bumpers, power-up stars
  and bank-shot bonuses; 2 players on one screen still works. Rivals softened for kids, faster
  keyboard mallet.
- **Asteroid Storm:** waves of splitting rocks with crystal power-ups (shield, triple shot, extra
  ship), scout bots, a mega rock every 5th wave, score combos up to x5 and touch-to-aim controls.
- **Block Drop:** climb 10 levels with star blocks that win bombs, rising rubble, HOLD, a two-piece
  NEXT preview, combos and a real ending after level 10.
- **Boat Rush:** race five themed river stages from Meadow to Sunny Delta, dodging logs,
  whirlpools and islands, with coin and buoy-gate multipliers, boosts and extra-life hearts.
- **Brick Breaker:** 8 hand-made levels with steel, bomb, stone and sliding-bumper bricks, six
  power-ups, combo multipliers and a real ending; every level starts with at least 3 balls.
- **Bubble Blaster:** 12 levels with stars, stones and chain-reaction bombs, a rainbow bubble,
  bubble swapping, a bounce-aware aim line and 3 hearts instead of instant game over.
- **Castle Climb:** climb 8 castle towers to win the crown, with a locked door and key, spikes,
  bats, fast blue flames, hearts, a time bonus and respawning on the floor where you fell.
- **Cave Explorer:** escape 8 dark caves with a lamp that needs oil, rubies, treasure chests,
  pop-up spikes, a red bat that hunts you, hearts and a speed bonus.
- **Frog Crossing:** endless levels that each add something new (diving turtles, trucks, a fast
  racer, a snake), a time bar, a bonus fly and ladybug, extra lives and hops that bump instead of
  killing you.
- **Gem Match:** endless levels with goals to collect, rocks to smash, frozen gems, arrow and
  burst power gems, keyboard play, hints and smooth animations.
- **Hoop Shot:** 8 rounds of hoops that get trickier (higher, sliding, windy, bumper and bobbing
  hoops); baskets give your ball back, misses in the warm-up round are free.
- **Jungle Run:** a 6-stage jungle adventure from morning to firefly night with bats, frogs,
  thorns, drifting logs, bouncy mushrooms, bop combos, a hidden gold star per stage, and a heart
  back at each flag.
- **Kart Cup:** a four-track cup (meadow, beach, snow and moon) with coins for turbo boosts, boost
  pads, oil slicks and cones; you are only out after finishing last twice.
- **Maze Chase:** clear 8 colourful mazes with four slimes that each chase in their own way, ice
  crystals that freeze them, portals, bonus fruit, combos and PERFECT bonuses.
- **Memory Cards:** 7 levels of growing boards with a golden eye that reveals every card, a golden
  heart that gives a life back and sneaky cards that swap places; level 1 is a free practice level.
- **Mini Golf:** 9 named holes with a par each, sand, ponds, bumpers, sliding gates, speed strips,
  warp pads, stars to collect and a scorecard at the end.
- **Moto Hills:** ride through 5 lands to the checkered flag, flying off ramps, flipping for points
  and grabbing fuel, with 3 helmets to spare.
- **Ninja Dash:** dash across 8 night rooftops to the gold gate, jumping and sliding past a new
  trap every stage, with 3 hearts and coin streaks up to x5.
- **Number Slide:** reach the goal tile on each level for bonus points, extra undos and a
  swept-clean board, with star tiles that double numbers and rocks in your way.
- **Paddle Duel:** battle five rival paddles across five courts for the cup, with bumpers, sliding
  gates and power stars (BIG, ICE, TURBO), three hearts, and a friend who can take the top paddle.
- **Penalty Kick:** win the Falcon Cup over 8 rounds of 5 penalties against a keeper who sways and
  dives, with a bonus star target, a wall of dummies, wind and a wobbly aim in later rounds.
- **Pipe Flow:** boards grow from 4x4 to 7x7 with clocks, coins, bolted pipes, icy pipes and a
  moving pump; earn up to 3 stars, watch flowers bloom, and play with the keyboard.
- **Robo Jump:** climb 600 m through five sky zones to the Star Station, with 3 hearts, moving and
  crumbling platforms, buzz-bugs to stomp, bolts and rocket batteries.
- **Sky Defender:** defend six towers through waves of splitters, drones, steel bombs, rockets and
  a storm blimp boss every 5th wave, with parachute power-ups and chain combos.
- **Snake Trail:** guide your snake through 9 gardens: eat every apple, slide into the burrow, dodge
  rocks and ladybugs and build combos, with 3 hearts.
- **Space Slalom:** race the 8-sector Galaxy Cup through gates for streaks and PERFECT passes,
  dodging rocks and comets, catching shields and hearts, to the checkered line.
- **Table Tennis:** battle five rivals for the Table Tennis Cup with smashes that score double,
  curve balls, bonus stars, a rally counter and three hearts.
- **Tank Patrol:** defend your flag base through 10 missions against four kinds of enemy tank, with
  steel walls, water, bushes and five power-ups.
- **Turbo Road:** race 6 themed stages from Sunny Meadow to a Night Rush, with coins, nitro bolts
  that smash through traffic, trucks, oil, ice, road works and cars that change lanes (from stage 2).

### Platform
- **Pause button:** a Pause/Resume button next to Restart; the pause panel has RESUME and Restart.
  P and auto-pause on a hidden tab still work. While paused, keys no longer reach the game.
- **Home rows:** a Continue Playing row with your recently played games, next to Favourites.
- **Cards:** every card shows your best score and has a star to favourite it straight from the home
  page (including Star Falcon). Card blurbs were refreshed for the reworked games.
- **Game over:** a blinking NEW BEST! with pixel confetti and a jingle, or your place in the top 5,
  or how many points you need to get in; the new row is highlighted in the High Scores table.
- **Your stats:** tap your name to see your rank, games tried, rounds played, time played and top
  games.
- **Keyboard:** a skip link, clearer focus outlines, "/" to search, Enter to open the first match
  and arrow keys to move between cards.
- **Phones:** a one-line swipeable category bar, full-width search, a shorter hero and safe-area
  insets for notched screens. The Full screen button hides where the browser cannot do it.
- **Home screen and sharing:** a web app manifest and falcon icons for adding the arcade to the home
  screen, per-page titles, a meta description and link-preview tags.
- **Text:** the site font no longer joins "fi", "fl" and "ff" into one wrong letter (also fixed in
  Star Falcon), and the score bar above a game keeps SCORE and BEST on one line.
- **Saves:** old v0.3 saves load unchanged; new optional fields are `plays`, `recent` and `time`.
  Damaged saves no longer break the game-over screen.
- **Star Falcon art:** the three Star Falcon pictures on the home page were re-shot from v2.0.

### Known issues
- **Star Falcon:**
  - The boss bar covers the top few pixels of some bosses when they sit highest (Orbit Warden,
    Magma Forge, The Prism).
  - Normal difficulty stays forgiving for fully upgraded players on stages 5-8.
  - Boss Rush pays a lot of credits (about 21,700 $ per run); it only unlocks after stage 8.
  - Pressing Pause during the resume countdown lets one more frame run.
  - On the menu and results screens the thumb bar still shows BOMB and Pause, which do nothing there.
  - On phones the menu scrolls: the stage grid, How to play and Erase sit below the fold.
  - The results tally assumes 60 fps, so it reveals more slowly on 30 Hz screens.
  - Score popups can draw over the $ counter, and at small sizes the font's "5" can look like "8".
  - The arcade home page does not read Star Falcon's own save, so its card shows no best score.
- **Games:**
  - Air Hockey rounds 3-5 are still hard for young children, and one lost match ends the cup.
  - Difficulty was tuned with test bots. Pipe Flow (from level 7), Mini Golf (holes 6 and 9),
    Maze Chase, Sky Defender and Memory Cards (levels 5-7 may be too easy) still need a check by
    real children.
  - Mini Golf has no lose state: a hole abandoned at 8 putts scores 0.
  - 12 game files are above the 16 KiB size target; the largest are Turbo Road (21 KiB),
    Tank Patrol (20 KiB), Mini Golf (19 KiB) and Cave Explorer (18 KiB).
  - Penalty Kick and Hoop Shot jingles use timers, so a note can play just after leaving the game.
  - Shared `K.fx` particles keep moving for a moment while a game is paused.
- **Platform:**
  - `og:image` is a relative URL, so some link previews may not show the picture.
  - Nothing has been tested on a real iOS or Android device, only in phone-sized browser windows.
